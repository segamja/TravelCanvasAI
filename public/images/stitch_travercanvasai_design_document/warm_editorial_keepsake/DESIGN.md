## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, sensory nostalgia, and refined craftsmanship of luxury travel journals and contemporary photobooks. It is built for mindful travelers, documentarians, and storytellers who wish to reconstruct fragmented memories into coherent, artful narratives without technical friction. 

The aesthetic marries **Tactile Editorial Minimalist** sensibilities with gentle, human-centric design. Rather than stark tech-forward interfaces, the visual tone evokes heavy off-white paper, sun-baked clay, sea breezes, and physical binding. The interface recedes quietly behind photography, presenting generous white space, organic earth-derived color accents, and bookmaker proportions that grant travel memories dignified visual weight. Every interaction feels deliberate, calm, and tactile—treating photographs as permanent artifacts rather than transient digital feed items.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from Mediterranean clay, coastal waters, and sunlit linen.

- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Terracotta):** Represents physical earth, kiln-fired pottery, and golden hour sunsets. It serves as the primary action and focus trigger across key user milestones.
- **Secondary (`#2A7B88` - Horizon Teal):** Balances warm clay with the coolness of deep maritime waters and open horizons, applied to discovery tags, timeline anchors, and navigation indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#E59838` - Warm Amber):** Evokes candlelight and sunlight sparkles, used specifically for AI memory milestones, interactive interview sparks, and subtle progress cues.
- **Neutral (`#5C5854` - Warm Slate):** A tinted neutral carrying warmth from stone and natural earth pigments rather than synthetic grays.
- **Background & Canvas (`#FAFAF7`):** Mimics the texture of unbound archival cotton paper, shielding against eye fatigue and framing digital photos as physical prints.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere that alternates between literary charm and crisp, functional cataloging.

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Dedicated to story-level focal points, major journey chapters, dates, and poetic section divides. Its high stroke contrast brings bespoke magazine prestige to personal narratives.
- **Utilitarian Sans (Inter):** Serves body copy, conversational AI prompt dialogues, EXIF tags, and interface controls. Generous line-height (`1.75` on editorial body tokens) guarantees prolonged reading comfort, ensuring extended travel memoirs flow seamlessly.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** Story and page headlines gracefully compress on mobile viewports to prevent awkward word wraps and preserve natural visual cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an editorial 12-column grid system designed to simulate printed spread compositions rather than rigid dashboard containers.

- **Desktop (1200px+ max-width):** 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `2.5rem` outer margins. Content spreads utilize asymmetric spans (e.g., 7 columns for a focal photo paired with 5 columns of text and meta cards).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 2-column image pairings and single-column interview flows.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout where image blocks bleed to the margins, and margins compress to `1.25rem` to prioritize touch targets and full-width imagery.
- **Rhythm:** Inter-section spacing commands large intervals (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to establish natural pauses between consecutive travel days or scenes, giving every memory distinct chapter boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop shadows and intense glass blurs, favoring warm, low-contrast diffuse shadows that recreate physical photobooks resting on woven linen surfaces.

- **Flat Canvas:** Base canvas `#FAFAF7` sits flush, grounding the experience with zero elevation.
- **Photobook Tactile Shadow:** Utilizes a double-layered brown-tinted shadow matrix (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(40, 30, 20, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(40, 30, 20, 0.03)`), giving white picture cards and albums soft separation without artificial glare.
- **Floating Keepsakes:** Dialogues, memory questionnaires, and image dropzones apply elevated ambient warmth (`0 14px 34px -4px rgba(35, 25, 15, 0.09)`) combined with a fine 1px paper-edge border (`border-subdued`).
- **Layering Philosophy:** Depth is primarily reinforced through tonal surface stepping: moving from warm canvas (`#FAFAF7`) to subtle container (`#F3F2EE`) to pure card surface (`#FFFFFF`).

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced `roundedness: 2` (base 8px / 0.5rem) standard. This softens clean geometric edges into an inviting, approachable form that still retains clean editorial structure.

- **Cards & Photobook Pages:** Bound with `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) to evoke round-cut luxury paper goods.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Set to `rounded-md` (10px) to offer distinct, confident touch boundaries.
- **Tags, Indicators & Micro Badges:** Use full pill shapes (`rounded-full`) to differentiate functional metadata from rectangular image compositions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#C85A32` (Terracotta) background with `#FFFFFF` text, subtle shadow, and 10px rounded corners. On hover, shifts to `#B34E29` with a gentle `-1px` vertical lift.
- **Secondary / Horizon:** Tinted sea-teal `#2A7B88` or soft background `#F0F8F9` with teal label for secondary discoveries.
- **Ghost / Editorial:** Pure transparent background with `#1E1E1E` text, subtle underline animation, and generous text spacing.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Rendered with `#F3F2EE` background, `#5C5854` warm slate text, and fine tracking.
- **Active / Selection Chips:** High-contrast terracotta-tinted border (`#C85A32`), tinted warm wash `#FDF5F2`, and bold label for interactive choices.

### Memory Interview Bubble & Cards
- **Conversational Cards:** Clean `#FFFFFF` container with `rounded-lg` (16px), bordered in `#E5E3DC`.
- **Dialogue Prompt:** Introduces thumbnail references of relevant scenes alongside a warm prompt query and quick-select response pills for low-effort input.

### Project & Chapter Photobook Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 4:3 or 3:2 aspect ratio preview picture container framed by paper-white backing and subtle brown-tinted shadow.
- **Details:** Includes serif chapter indicator, EXIF metadata badge (photo count, location, date span), and smooth hover lift transition (`transform: translateY(-4px)`).

### Input Fields & Zero-Drop Photo Zone
- **Dropzone:** Dashed 1.5px border in `#D1CEB8` over `#F3F2EE` background, accented by a warm amber camera motif and encouraging copy.
- **Inputs:** Warm off-white inputs (`#FFFFFF`) with `#E5E3DC` borders that shift to focused terracotta outlines (`#C85A32`) with zero jarring blue rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom rounded squares (checkbox) and circles (radio) tinted in `#C85A32` upon selection, with clean white interior glyphs.
